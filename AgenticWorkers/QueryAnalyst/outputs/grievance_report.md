The grievance has a significant impact on the environment, affecting both land and a water source. The presence of garbage in the water body poses a risk to public health and the ecosystem. The severity of the issue and its potential consequences warrant a high priority level for resolution. The responsible department is the Public Works Department (PWD) or Municipal Corporation, and supporting departments may include the local health department and environmental agency. Immediate action is required to mitigate the risks and restore the affected area to a safe and healthy state. Short-term corrective measures may include clearing the garbage, repairing the metal grid fence, and ensuring regular garbage collection and disposal services. Long-term measures may involve implementing education and awareness programs about proper waste management, improving waste collection infrastructure, and enforcing stricter regulations against open dumping and pollution. The case requires immediate attention to prevent further environmental degradation and potential health risks.
